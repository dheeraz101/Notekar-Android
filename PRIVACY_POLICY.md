# Privacy Policy: NoteKar Android

NoteKar Android is engineered with an **offline-first and privacy-focused architecture**.

## Highlights

- 🔒 **Local-First Storage:** All timestamps, moments, notes, tags, and preferences stay on your device via local Isar database and `SharedPreferences`. No database passwords or records leave your device.
- 🌐 **No Account or Cloud Log Sync:** NoteKar operates without user accounts, analytics trackers, or remote log-storage servers.
- 💾 **System Auto-Backup Support:** Standard Android OS Google Drive system backup is supported for device transfer and data protection if enabled by the user in Android Settings.
- 📡 **Transparent Network Access:**
  - `INTERNET`: Used strictly to fetch public software release metadata, version checks, and bulletin feeds from GitHub.
  - Zero user-authored logs, notes, or personal metrics are ever transmitted in network requests.
- 🚫 **Zero Third-Party Trackers or Ads:** No commercial ad networks, third-party analytics SDKs, or profiling tools are embedded in the app.
