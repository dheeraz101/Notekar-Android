## 🚨 Priority Release — NoteKar v7.5.0 (`26PR0908a`)

> *"NoteKar has revisioned itself."* A sovereign tool does not merely log time; it honors the truth
> of human history. This Priority Update hardens our zero-tracking notice architecture across 7
> languages, calibrates existential boundaries to eliminate phantom void calculations, and unifies
> modal navigation into an unbroken flow.

---

### What's New

#### 🛡️ Hardened Official Bulletins & Notice Center

- **Complete 7-Language Parity**: Full offline native dictionaries and dynamic resolution across
  English, Hindi (with Devanagari digits), Spanish, German, French, Japanese, and Russian for all
  notices and system advisories.
- **Hierarchical Priority Categories**: Explicit architecture for Critical Advisories, Security
  Advisories (with dedicated shield geometry), Release Bulletins, and Curated Tips.
- **Offline-First Tactile Feedback**: Eliminated disruptive modal alert dialogs; manual refresh
  while offline floats an Apple-style tactile iOS pill toast (`showIosPillToast`).
- **Silent Freshness Sync & Cache Self-Healing**: Stale bulletins (> 4 hours) sync quietly in the
  background; malformed local caches automatically purge and restore defaults.
- **Context-Aware Smart Routing**: Action buttons intelligently adapt their labels ("View
  Release", "Open Life Audit", "Go to Settings", "Open History") and icons with strict 6-second
  timeout guards.

#### ⚖️ Life Ledger History Calibration

- **Boundary-Anchored Wastage Engine**: Existential void hours and conscious waking loss metrics are
  strictly bounded to the user's recorded tracking history, eliminating edge-case phantom void hours
  on new or variable-history accounts.
- **Symmetrical Cost of Void Layout**: Equalized dimensions and visual proportions for "Waking Days
  Lost" and "Earth Days Lost" subcards.
- **Anti-Collision Typography**: Refined header spacing and text containers to guarantee clean
  negative space and zero text clipping across all screen densities.

#### 🧭 Universal Settings Navigation & Sheet Unification

- **Integrated Category Stack**: Official Bulletins is now a first-class citizen inside the Settings
  navigation hierarchy with full back-button parity.
- **Universal Modal Back Handlers**: Added explicit `onBack` handlers to all modal dialogs,
  secondary inspection sheets, Milestone Peak celebrations, and reset workflows.
